Meghalaya

☁️

The Abode of Clouds

Meghalaya is a scenic state in Northeast India known for its lush green hills, misty valleys, cascading waterfalls, and living root bridges. Home to Shillong, Cherrapunji, and Mawsynram, it is famous for having some of the highest rainfall on Earth.

🏞️ Shillong

The capital city, often called the "Scotland of the East," famous for Umiam Lake and vibrant music culture.

🌉 Living Root Bridges

Hand-woven bridges grown from the roots of rubber fig trees in Nongriat and Cherrapunji.

💧 Dawki River

Known for its crystal-clear waters where boats appear to float in mid-air.
