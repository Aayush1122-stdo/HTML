Eiffel Tower

Eiffel Tower

Engineering and History

World's Fair: It was built for the 1889 World's Fair to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the French Revolution.

Gustave Eiffel: The tower was designed by the French engineer Gustave Eiffel and his company.

Height: The Eiffel Tower stands at a height of 324 meters (1,063 feet) and was the tallest

Iron Structure: It is made of iron and consists of over 18,000 individual iron parts held together by 2.5 million rivets.

Tourist Attraction: The Eiffel Tower is one of the most visited monuments in the world, attracting millions of tourists each year.

Taj Mahal

Taj Mahal

Architecture and Symbolism

Mughal Architecture: The Taj Mahal is a masterpiece of Mughal architecture, combining elements from Islamic, Persian, Ottoman Turkish, and Indian architectural styles.

White Marble: It is constructed using white marble inlaid with precious and semi-precious stones.

UNESCO World Heritage Site: The Taj Mahal was designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983 for being "the jewel of Muslim art in India and one of the universally admired masterpieces of the world's heritage."

Symbol of Love: It is often referred to as a symbol of love due to the romantic story behind its construction.

Gardens: The Taj Mahal is surrounded by beautiful gardens that enhance its aesthetic appeal.

Statue of Liberty

Statue of Liberty

Symbolism and History

Gift from France: The Statue of Liberty was a gift from the people of France to the United States in 1886 to commemorate the centennial of the American Declaration of Independence.

Symbol of Freedom: It is a symbol of freedom and democracy and has become an iconic representation of the United States.

Design: The statue was designed by French sculptor Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi and its metal framework was built by Gustave Eiffel.

Location: It is located on Liberty Island in New York Harbor and welcomes visitors arriving by sea.

National Monument: The Statue of Liberty was designated as a National Monument in 1924 and is managed by the National Park Service.

Great Wall of China

Great Wall of China

History and Construction

Ancient Defense: The Great Wall of China was built over several dynasties to protect Chinese states and empires from invasions and raids by nomadic groups from the north.

Length: The wall stretches over approximately 13,000 miles (21,196 kilometers) across northern China.

Construction Materials: It was constructed using various materials including earth, wood, bricks, and stone depending on the region and availability of resources.

UNESCO World Heritage Site: The Great Wall of China was designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987 for its historical significance and architectural grandeur.

Tourist Attraction: It is one of the most popular tourist attractions in China and draws millions of visitors from around the world each year.
